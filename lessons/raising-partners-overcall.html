When should I raise partner’s overcall?

When partner overcalls, even a modest hand may be worth a raise. You’ll decide whether three hearts and useful side honours are enough to support partner after a 2♥ overcall.

Improving club players · 15 minutes · Standard SAYC

Board 3 · Your call as East

South deals. East–West vulnerable.

South 1♠ (Introduce) → West 2♥ (overcall) → North Pass → East ?

♠ A9863 · ♥ JT3 · ♦ K73 · ♣ 65

Choose Pass, 3♥, or 2♠. Which of your cards are likely to help West take tricks? Are you willing to play 3♥ if West has a minimum?

Reveal the bidding discussion and recorded result

I favour 3♥, a natural, nonforcing raise. You have 8 HCP, three trumps headed by J–10, a side ace and a doubleton club. The ♦K may help, depending on West’s shape and where the ♦A lies. That is enough to consider raising a sound two-level overcall. Pass is defensible at adverse vulnerability if you judge the hand too marginal to risk the three level.

Rule of thumb. With three trumps opposite a sound two-level overcall, count useful trump honours and likely side winners. Raise when you are willing to play at the next level opposite a minimum. With stronger game interest, 2♠ is the SAYC cue-bid that forces a reply and asks about the quality of the overcall. East’s hand is light for that call. The booklet specifies the cue-bid treatment and leaves the exact strength of this simple 3♥ raise unstated. ACBL SAYC System Booklet

Common exception. When the support is poor and the side honours are unlikely to take tricks, passing can be right with three trumps. Vulnerability makes a marginal three-level raise less attractive.

Recorded result. East passed. West played 2♥ and took 11 tricks. West held 17 HCP, seven hearts headed by A–K–Q, and a diamond void. The partnership had ten trumps, and East’s ♦K faced a void.

Traveller. Eight of 16 tables bid and made 4♥. Six took eleven tricks and two took twelve. Those results describe this deal. At the decision, East had the auction, the eight high-card points and the SAYC meanings of the available calls to go on.

Variation · One more trump

Same auction and vulnerability. Replace the ♠3 with the ♥4.

♠ A986 · ♥ JT43 · ♦ K73 · ♣ 65

Does the fourth heart change your call or your confidence in it?

Reveal the variation

Bid 3♥. The fourth trump makes the natural raise more attractive at the same 8 HCP. You have better support even if West has only five hearts.

Fresh hand

Same auction and vulnerability.

♠ 74 · ♥ Q84 · ♦ A865 · ♣ KQ62

Which call asks West to describe the quality of the overcall? Which cards give you game interest?

Reveal the fresh hand

Bid 2♠. The trump queen, side ace and ♣KQ give you 11 HCP and useful support. The forcing cue-bid lets West return to 3♥ with a minimum. You can pass that rebid.

Independent check

After (1♠)–2♥–(Pass), what is the difference between 3♥ and 2♠ in SAYC?

Check your answer

3♥ is a natural, nonforcing heart raise. 2♠ cues the opener’s suit, forces a reply and asks about the quality of partner’s overcall.

Alternative Conventions

Some pairs agree that direct 3♥ after this exact auction is invitational. Others use a weak raise, often requiring four trumps, and reserve 2♠ for an invitational-or-better raise with support. Agree on both strength and trump length. Larry Cohen’s example of an invitational 3♥
