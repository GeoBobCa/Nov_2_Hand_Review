Open a strong hand with a seven-card major

A seven-card suit gives you two very different ways to start the auction. You’ll compare a one-level opening with a preempt and consider what each tells partner about your strength outside the long suit.

Board 10 · Standard SAYC

East deals and both sides are vulnerable. You are East, first to act.

♠ AK · ♥ KJT8743 · ♦ JT · ♣ A8

Choose your opening bid (Introduce). What would partner infer from 1♥ or 3♥?

Reveal the bidding discussion

Open 1♥. With 16 HCP, seven hearts and two outside aces, you want room for partner to describe their hand. A preemptive 3♥ opening invites partner to judge game facing a much weaker hand. After 1♥, use the next round to show your extra playing strength in light of partner’s response.

Explore. West actually held three hearts headed by the queen and the ♣K. Ask what West could communicate after 1♥, then consider East’s next call. Treat this as a bidding exercise with an open continuation.

Reveal the recorded result and traveller

East opened 3♥ and played there, taking ten tricks. Nine tables bid and made 4♥ exactly.

Check your understanding

Before opening a long major at the three level, name the outside winners partner would miss if they interpreted the opening as a preempt. Here they include the ♠A, ♠K and ♣A.
