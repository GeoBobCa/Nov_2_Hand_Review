November 2 with Bea and Bruno

16 recorded deals and 10 travellers.

Start with Boards 3 and 4. Compare a raise after an overcall with a passed-hand limit raise, then decide whether the partner who hears the raise should bid game.

Board 3 — Show support after partner overcalls

Bea passed over Bruno’s 2♥ with three hearts, the ♠A, the ♦K and 8 HCP. Bruno took eleven tricks in 2♥. Eight of sixteen tables bid and made 4♥. Compare pass with a natural 3♥ raise under standard SAYC, then replay Bruno’s decision if he hears that raise.

Board 4 — Decide whether to accept a limit raise

Georgena’s passed-hand 3♠ invites game in standard SAYC. Bob had 11 HCP, five spades headed by the jack and two cards in each minor. Pass 3♠ with that minimum. The recorded 4♠ went down three. All six tables in 3♠ or 4♠ went down.

Board 2 — Describe opener’s long suit

After 1♣ – 1♠, Bob rebid 1NT with 15 HCP, a singleton spade and six clubs. Compare 2♣ with 1NT and discuss what each tells Georgena. Four tables made 3NT and three made 5♣. The recorded 2♣ took eleven tricks.

Board 10 — Leave room to show a strong seven-card major

Bea opened 3♥ with 16 HCP, seven hearts, the ♠AK and the ♣A. With a standard preemptive 3♥ agreement, prefer 1♥ and leave room for Bruno to show support. Nine tables made 4♥ exactly.

Board 15 — Count dummy’s winners and entries

Pause after Georgena wins the ♥K in 3NT. Compare a heart return with a switch. From Bruno’s seat, identify the heart entry to Bea’s long suit. The recorded heart return gave Bruno time to reach dummy. The traveller for this board was unavailable in the packet.
